Welcome to MusicWidget

Publisher: Delexo
Website:  https://delexo.store

This installer will set up MusicWidget on your PC — a free floating desktop
music player for Windows 10 and 11.

What you get:
  • A minimal always-on-top widget for playback controls
  • Folder-based playlists under My Music\MusicWidget
  • Optional download from links you paste (YouTube, Spotify, and more)

No account is required. MusicWidget runs locally on your machine.
